Integrity System · ركائز الثقة الخمس

The 5 Pillars of Platform Integrity

Trust is not an assumed sentiment. It is an engineered property implemented through software verification, procedural accountability, and observable actions.

PILLAR 01 verified

Entity & Need Verification

Standing institutional requests must provide official accreditation (e.g., Ministry of Social Solidarity license numbers, accredited public school registries, municipal community kitchen hygiene certificates).

Verification Badge Rules: Only entities with audited field credentials display the public shield. Self-declarations remain unbadged until verified.
security Accredited Civil Partner Model
PILLAR 02 hub

Deterministic 5-Signal Matching

No black-box algorithms or opaque ranking. Dawwarha evaluates compatibility through 5 documented factors: Category (100% hard constraint), Geo-Radius (<5km), Quantity Capacity, Urgency tier, and Transport window.

Explainability Guarantee: Every suggested match displays the full mathematical breakdown directly in the provider dashboard.
code Rule-Based Civic Engine
PILLAR 03 qr_code_2

Dual-Party Handover (FR-013)

A resource is never logged as transferred simply because someone agreed in chat. The system strictly requires both recipient and provider to sign off physically using a dynamic OTP or mutual QR scan.

Anti-Ghosting Guard: Neither party can unilaterally mark a handover as completed, eliminating phantom pickups.
how_to_reg Cryptographic Presence Verification
PILLAR 04 report_problem

Polymorphic Moderation Queue

Any user can flag resources, requests, or entities for non-compliance, expired perishables, safety hazards, or commercial solicitation. Reports enter an auditable admin triage queue.

Enforcement Standard: Violators receive automatic temporary lockout and mandatory compliance review before re-entry.
gavel Accountable Community Oversight

No estimated CO2 trees or speculative financial valuations. Dawwarha records tangible, immutable metric entries: verified prepared meal units transferred within safe thermal limits, functioning refurbished workstations deployed to accredited learning labs, and hospital furniture placed into clinic service.

Trust Is a Process, Not an Ornamental Badge

We don't hand out vanity ribbons. Integrity is guaranteed through continuous validation checkpoints embedded at each step of the resource lifecycle.

1
Before Listing

Schema & Standard Validation

Mandatory condition grades, expiration time-stamping, allergen declaration, and institutional accreditation pre-checks.

• NFSA compliance check
• Serial number deduplication
2
During Matching

Shielded Coordination

Deterministic 5-signal matching. Direct personal contacts remain masked until both parties confirm operational terms.

• Mutual agreement lock
• Shielded communication
3
At Physical Handover

Two-Sided Custody

In-person verification via mutual dynamic QR code or OTP token. Handover dock confirmation recorded with time hash.

• Physical verification scan
• Good Samaritan liability shield
4
After Fulfillment

Ledger Accounting

Transfer converts into immutable record. Reputation score updates based solely on executed transfers, not reviews.

• Public cryptographic receipt
• Zero fake-review manipulation

Civic Philosophy · مبادئنا الأساسية

Four Principles That Guide Every Architecture Choice

cyclone

1. Useful Things Should Stay Useful

Surplus has genuine social value that is needlessly lost to landfills and decay because coordination friction makes giving harder than trashing. Dawwarha lowers transactional friction to zero.

view_agenda

2. Community Needs Deserve Structure

Desperation should not have to perform on social media algorithms to be seen. Accredited schools, shelters, and medical outposts need standardized, structured specifications that logicians can match efficiently.

visibility

3. Transparency Builds Enduring Trust

Every match compatibility score, queue position, custody status, and moderation resolution explains its reasoning. When users understand why an action occurs, platform faith becomes resilient.

stacked_bar_chart

4. Impact Should Be Measurable

Civic accountability demands factual outcomes. We count physical units delivered, thermal standards maintained, and verified requests closed — rejecting PR hype and unverified claims.

Governance & Oversight · الرقابة المجتمعية

Civic infrastructure requires active safeguarding. Our moderation framework combines polymorphic community reporting with documented administrative oversight.

Users can immediately report non-compliant items across 4 categories: Expired/Unsafe Perishables, Inaccurate Descriptions, Commercial Resale Suspicion, and Handover No-Shows.

timer

FIFO Admin Resolution Queue

Any attempt to solicit payment, broker transactions, or divert verified community goods into commercial markets results in permanent account suspension and partner notification.
