A predictable, transparent 5-stage procedure designed to eliminate guesswork, prevent wasted transit, and ensure physical safety.

STEP 03 Deterministic Match (التوافق الخوارزمي)

Explainable 5-Signal Compatibility Engine

Dawwarha does NOT use black-box neural ranking or opaque ad models. Every match calculation is deterministic and transparent to both parties, weighted across five immutable civic criteria.

1
Category Alignment (100% hard barrier): Item specifications must fit exact recipient constraints (e.g. halal prepared, grade-school dimensions).
2
Proximity & Transit Corridor: Distance evaluated along actual road arteries (Cairo Ring Road, 6th October Bridge) to keep transit under 45 mins.
3
Quantity Compatibility Ratio: Surplus batch quantity mapped against recipient storage capacity to prevent intermediate dumping.
4
Urgency & Shelf-Life Sync: Matches perishables before thermal expiration thresholds elapse (<4h safe consumption).
5
Schedule & Dock Synchrony: Provider loading dock hours synchronized with recipient intake staff presence.

The 5 Civic Trust Gates

Ensuring that mutual community aid operates with enterprise-grade legal, hygienic, and security protections.

G1

Entity Verification

All receiving NGOs, clinics, and community kitchens undergo cross-referencing with Ministry of Social Solidarity (MOSS) registries. Commercial entities seeking tax documentation provide valid commercial registration.

verified_user MOSS & Tax ID Audited
G2

Sensitive Item Protocols

Perishables adhere to Egyptian National Food Safety Authority (NFSA) thermal criteria. Hot cooked batches require logs demonstrating maintenance at >63°C, or rapid chill below <4°C within 120 minutes.

thermostat Thermal Chain Enforcement
G3

Two-Sided Confirmation

Unilateral claims are structurally impossible. Both the dispatcher and the receiver must validate the physical transaction on-device using a cryptographic key pair before the system updates status.

G4

Anti-Ghosting Safeguards

Community organizers have zero patience for no-shows. Accounts that commit to handovers and fail to dispatch within the scheduled window face immediate matching throttle and account review.

Every handover event writes to an append-only civic ledger. Community stakeholders, civic observers, municipal partners, and participating NGOs can audit aggregate distribution metrics without exposing private beneficiary addresses.

Frequently Asked Civic Questions

Clear, unambiguous answers about how Dawwarha functions in the field.

Strictly zero. Dawwarha is an open civic public utility. All items, furniture, ingredients, and durable goods offered through the protocol are 100% free. Any party attempting to demand side-payments, delivery markups, or deposit fees will be permanently expelled from the registry and reported to partner networks.
We enforce strict compliance with Egyptian National Food Safety Authority (NFSA) thermal parameters. Prepared hot food must be logged with dispatch temperature (>63°C), transferred in insulated containers, and accepted by recipient kitchens within a strict 120-minute transit window for immediate consumption or approved rapid blast freezing.
To protect resource integrity and prevent predatory resale, request rights are granted exclusively to verified entities: registered Egyptian non-governmental organizations (MOSS licensed), community schools, charitable clinics, senior care residences, and accredited neighborhood coordinators whose facilities have undergone physical or remote verification.
Recipients are under strict operational instructions to reject any batch that exhibits hygiene concerns, safety defects, or significant variance from the initial listing. Rejecting a handoff triggers an automated "Quality Variance" report and does NOT penalize the recipient’s reliability rating.
